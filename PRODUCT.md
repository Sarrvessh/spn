# Capsule

Private prompt and file sharing for individuals, with confidential collection
forms as a secondary workflow. Browser encryption and fragment keys are existing
product foundations. White backgrounds are a user requirement.

Prioritize quick sharing, personal exchange history, and encrypted backup/recovery.
Keep existing prompt structure, collection fields, limits, and recipient routes.
Accounts, direct delivery, notifications, and encrypted cloud backup are implemented integrations requiring operator configuration. Authentication and account settings have dedicated routes; anonymous local sharing stays optional. Teams and billing are outside the individual-sharing product scope.
